# ICP Definition

Last updated: 2026-04-19

## Ideal Customer Profile

### Firmographics

| Dimension | Ideal Range | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Employee count | 1–30 | Early-stage startup or boutique agency; founder is still the face of the brand |
| Revenue (if known) | $0–$5M ARR | Big enough to afford a ~$1k/mo retainer, small enough that founder brand is the growth lever |
| Funding stage | Bootstrapped, Pre-seed, Seed, early Series A | Founders still personally doing outbound and content |
| Funding recency | Raised in last 12 months OR growing profitably | Either signals cash for discretionary marketing |
| Industry | B2B SaaS, B2B services, AI/data tooling, dev tools, GTM tech, B2B agencies | Categories where LinkedIn is the primary distribution channel |
| Geography | **Europe and US** (primary) | Samy is EU-based — time zone and cultural proximity matter for the retainer relationship |
| Business model | B2B — sells to other companies, not consumers | LinkedIn-heavy buying committee |
| Founder profile | **Experienced founder** (not first-time), has shipped a product or agency before | Experienced founders recognize production quality and are less prone to shopping on price |

### Technographics

**Strong fit indicators:**
- Founder is **already posting on LinkedIn inconsistently with low engagement** — belief is there, leverage isn't (core ICP signal)
- Uses LinkedIn Sales Navigator or outbound tooling (Apollo, Clay, Instantly)
- Has a personal website, Substack, or prior content footprint — signals they invest in brand
- Founder has a small-but-real following (500–5K) — ceiling high, floor real

**Weak fit indicators:**
- Already retains a ghostwriter or content agency they're happy with
- Founder has never posted on LinkedIn — they don't believe in the channel (anti-ICP)
- Founder posts constantly with high engagement — they don't need us, they're the competition
- Uses LinkedIn only for recruiting, not GTM

### Organizational signals

- Founder is actively doing sales themselves (no full-time AE/SDR hire yet)
- No in-house designer or brand lead
- Hiring for growth/marketing/content roles — signals awareness but no internal capacity yet
- Solid, defined business with a clear offer (not a side project, not a pre-revenue idea)

## Tier Definitions

### Tier 1 — Dream Accounts (Top 150)

**Criteria:**
- Experienced B2B founder (prior operator, second-time founder, or agency owner with track record)
- 5–30 employees, Seed–Series A, or a profitable bootstrapped agency
- Posts on LinkedIn inconsistently (3–10 posts/quarter) with low-to-moderate engagement (sub-100 reactions avg)
- 1K–10K LinkedIn followers — real audience but clearly under the ceiling
- Europe or US based
- Solid, defined business — clear offer, clear ICP, clear revenue (not a stealth or pre-product shop)

**Outreach approach:** Manual, founder-to-founder DM from Samy. Deeply personalized around their recent content or offer. Lead with proximity ("I'm one person, one Slack away, not a queue behind a creator"), speed, and a visual mockup.

---

### Tier 2 — High-Fit Accounts (500–1,000)

**Criteria:**
- B2B founder or agency owner, 1–50 employees, Europe or US
- Posts on LinkedIn at least monthly but inconsistently
- Clear defined offer and ICP
- No visible existing content agency retainer

**Outreach approach:** Signal-triggered LinkedIn DM + email sequence. Reference a specific recent post. Lead with a carousel mockup of one of their existing posts as proof of quality.

---

### Tier 3 — Good-Fit Accounts (1,000–3,000)

**Criteria:**
- B2B founder or agency owner in Europe / US
- Has a LinkedIn profile with at least occasional activity (posts in last 6 months)
- Sells a defined B2B product or service

**Outreach approach:** Automated LinkedIn + email sequence, template-based with one variable hook.

---

### Tier 4 — Monitor Only

First-time founders still finding product-market fit, pre-revenue solo builders, anyone not actively posting, anyone outside Europe/US for now.

## Anti-ICP

| Exclusion | Reason |
|-----------|--------|
| **Creators without a solid business** (personal brand only, no product/service behind it) | No real offer means no lead gen target; audience without product = no retainer budget sustainability |
| **Creators or founders with weak online presence** (sub-500 followers, never posted, no digital footprint) | We amplify; we don't manufacture from zero. Without any belief or footprint, the retainer won't survive the first month |
| **Founders who do not post on LinkedIn at all** | Core deal-breaker. Even the best production team can't fake a founder voice. They need to show up to review/approve at minimum |
| B2C / DTC / consumer brands | Wrong channel — LinkedIn isn't their growth lever |
| Agencies and content studios selling similar services | Competitors or substitutes — they build this in-house |
| Enterprise (500+ employees) | Founder is no longer the face; decisions go through marketing committees; procurement cycles too long for a ~$1k/mo retainer |
| Pre-product founders with no ICP defined | Lead gen has no target; content has no story |
| First-time founders with no track record | At $800–$1k/mo retainer, we need fast judgment from the buyer — experienced founders qualify themselves; first-timers over-deliberate |

## Qualification Framework

### Must-have (deal-breaker if absent)
1. B2B product, service, or agency with a definable ICP and real revenue
2. **Founder already posts on LinkedIn** (even inconsistently — willingness to show up is non-negotiable)
3. Experienced founder — has shipped before, can recognize production quality
4. Based in Europe or US

### Strong indicators (2+ = high confidence)
1. Posts are inconsistent with low engagement — exactly our core unlock
2. 1K–10K followers — audience big enough to matter, small enough to grow
3. Solid profitable business or Seed/Series A funded
4. Currently doing sales themselves and feeling the grind
5. Clear ICP — they can name the exact job title + company type they sell to
6. No existing content/SDR agency retainer

### Red flags (2+ = deprioritize)
1. Founder hasn't posted in 6+ months
2. First-time founder still searching for ICP
3. Price objection in the first message (at $800–$1k, we're already the value option — price shoppers will churn)
4. Wants a one-off project, not a retainer
5. Expects leads to close themselves — not willing to do sales
