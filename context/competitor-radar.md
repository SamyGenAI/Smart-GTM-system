## Agencies Led by Big Creators (PRIMARY LOSS PATTERN)

*LinkedIn agencies run by founders with 100K+ followers. They sell partly on service, largely on the halo of the creator's audience.*

**One-line summary:** "Work with [Big Creator] and get a piece of what they've built" — service layer wrapped around a personal brand.
**Market position:** Challenger with strong brand gravity. The default alternative a founder considers when they see our pitch.
**Pricing:** [inferred] $2k–$10k/month, higher than Igentiv
**Typical buyer:** B2B founders who've been following the big creator for months and want "whatever made them successful."

**Their strengths (be honest):**
- Brand halo — the creator's audience is implicit proof
- Distribution engine — the creator will sometimes amplify client content
- Established process, team, and portfolio
- "Safe" choice — easy to defend internally ("we hired [Big Creator]")

**Their weaknesses:**
- Founder buyer rarely talks to the big creator directly after the sale — you get a junior account manager
- Queue depth: 30–50+ clients means slow turnaround and template-driven output
- Quality per dollar drops as the agency scales — the creator is optimizing for volume, not craft
- The creator's voice bleeds into client work — posts start to look like the creator's, not the client's

**Why customers switch to us (or choose us over them):**
- They realized after a month at the big agency that they never talk to the actual creator
- Turnaround was 2+ weeks for a carousel revision
- Output felt generic, not tailored to their specific voice and ICP
- Price was 3–5x ours and they didn't feel the difference

**How to handle "I was thinking about [Big Creator Agency]" objection:**
> "Good shortlist. Honest question: are you buying their name, or are you buying the work? If you're buying the name, they're the right choice. If you're buying the work, ask them how many clients one account manager handles and how long a revision takes. Then compare. We're one person, one Slack, turnaround in days."

**How to position against them in cold outreach:**
- Lead with proximity: "I'm the founder. You won't talk to a junior."
- Lead with speed: "Turnaround in days, not weeks."
- Lead with quality per dollar: "$800/mo for the same level of craft as agencies charging $4k."
- Never attack the big creator personally — respect the brand, reframe the choice.

**Signal that an account is evaluating them:**
- Founder comments on or reshares the big creator's content
- Founder follows 3+ big-creator-agency founders
- Founder posts about "looking for a LinkedIn agency" — they've likely already looked at the big ones

**Competitive message for Tier 1 outreach:**
> "You've probably looked at [category of big-creator agencies] — they're good, and they're expensive, and you'll be client 47 in their pipeline. I'm one founder running a small studio; direct access, faster turnaround, fraction of the price. Worth a 15-min compare?"

## Traditional LinkedIn Ghostwriting Agencies

*(Skii, Superpath-style shops, independent ghostwriters who've productized)*

**One-line summary:** Writer-first agencies that produce LinkedIn text posts and occasionally light graphics for founders and execs.
**Market position:** Incumbent — this is the default category a founder considers first.
**Pricing:** [inferred] $2k–$6k/month retainer
**Typical buyer:** B2B founder or exec who wants to post more but can't write consistently.

**Their strengths (be honest):**
- Established rosters of writers with voice-matching skills
- Mature editorial workflows — briefs, revisions, publishing
- Some have strong brand/category recognition among founders

**Their weaknesses:**
- Text-only or text-plus-basic-graphics — the visual bar is low
- No lead gen — they hand you posts, not pipeline
- Output feels interchangeable at the visual level; every post looks like every other ghostwritten post
- Scaling cost linearly with writers; can't use AI efficiency without quality hit

**Why customers switch to us:**
- They hit the visual ceiling of text-only posts and want carousels/infographics
- They realize impressions don't become pipeline on their own
- They're tired of managing a writer AND chasing leads separately

**How to handle "we already have a ghostwriter" objection:**
> "Makes sense — text is table stakes. The founders we work with usually keep the writer and bolt us on for the visual layer and leads. Carousels get 3x the reach of text, and when the founder is already posting consistently, that multiplier shows up fast."

**Signal that an account uses them:**
- Founder posts are text-heavy, high-volume, formatted with the "ghostwriter tells" (line breaks, em dashes, one-word lines)
- Founder's feed has a sudden jump in post frequency in a given month

**Competitive message for Tier 1 outreach:**
> "Saw your last few posts — the cadence is dialed. Wanted to send you an example of what the same ideas look like as a carousel, if you're curious where the ceiling sits."

## Design-Only LinkedIn Agencies

*(Boutique design shops that specialize in carousels and infographics for founders)*

**One-line summary:** Premium carousel design studios; usually 2–5 person teams with strong visual portfolios.
**Market position:** Niche challenger — growing category as founders realize design matters.

**Their strengths:**
- High visual quality — genuinely good design
- Strong portfolios, usually with recognizable founder clients
- Focused scope — they do one thing well

**Their weaknesses:**
- No lead gen bundled — founder still has to solve pipeline separately
- No content strategy or voice — they execute briefs, not direction
- Typically smaller teams = capacity constraints

**Why customers switch to us:**
- They want pipeline alongside content
- They want someone who owns the strategy, not just the execution
- They're managing a design agency AND an SDR agency and want to consolidate

**How to handle "we already have a design agency" objection:**
> "Good — the hard part is design. Most founders we talk to have a design partner and still don't have pipeline. The reason we bundled is that the same ICP research that drives the content also drives the leads; running them together is where the leverage comes from."

**Signal that an account uses them:**
- Founder's carousels have a consistent branded style (not Canva templates)
- Same visual signature across multiple posts over months

## DIY Stack (Founder + AI Tools)

**One-line summary:** Founder using ChatGPT, Canva, Apollo, and Instantly to build their own brand + outbound stack.
**Market position:** The biggest competitor in aggregate — most founders try this first.

**Their strengths:**
- Zero vendor cost beyond tool subscriptions
- Full control over voice and ICP
- Fast iteration if the founder is technical enough

**Their weaknesses:**
- Quality bar is AI-generic — posts and visuals look like everyone else's AI output
- Founder time is the scarcest resource, and this consumes the most of it
- Usually abandoned after 4–8 weeks when the founder burns out

**Why customers switch to us:**
- They tried it, the output was embarrassing, they stopped posting
- They calculated the opportunity cost of their own time and it's wildly negative
- They want the premium ceiling that generic AI tools can't hit

**How to handle "I'll just use AI myself" objection:**
> "Same tools, different output. The reason it's different is taste — which AI doesn't have yet. Happy to send a comparison: same brief, AI alone vs. our workflow. You'll see the gap in 30 seconds."

**Signal that an account uses them:**
- Founder posts have distinctly AI-generated feel (generic hooks, stock phrasing)
- Canva-template carousels or inconsistent visual branding
- Posting cadence is bursty — high for 2 weeks, then silence

## Win Patterns

**vs. Big-creator-led agencies:** We win when the founder values direct access over brand halo — typically experienced founders who've been burned by "famous vendor, junior execution" before. Also win when price matters (we're 3–5x cheaper) and when the founder wants speed.

**vs. Ghostwriting agencies:** We win when the founder already knows text-only has plateaued and wants design + leads bundled. Content-first buyers stay; outcome-first buyers move to us.

**vs. Design-only agencies:** We win when the founder is also feeling pipeline pain. If they only care about visuals, they'll stay with specialists.

**vs. DIY:** We win after the founder's own attempt stalls (usually 2–3 months in). We lose if we catch them in the enthusiasm window.

---

## Loss Patterns

**vs. Big-creator-led agencies:** We lose when the founder is buying brand proof — they want to say "we work with [Big Creator]." Also lose when the founder hasn't yet learned that "the creator" ≠ "the person who actually produces your content." Reframe: show them one of the big agency's generic carousels next to one of our bespoke ones.

**vs. Ghostwriting agencies:** We lose when the founder is text-first, has a strong writer relationship, and sees carousels as optional. Also lose when budget is tight and they can only afford one of (content, leads).

**vs. Design-only agencies:** We lose when the founder has in-house sales/SDR and only wants the visual layer — they'd rather pay less for a narrower scope.

**vs. DIY:** We lose when the founder overestimates their own time or is too early-stage to justify the retainer. Coming back 3 months later is the pattern.
