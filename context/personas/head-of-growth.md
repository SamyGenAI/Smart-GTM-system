# Persona: Head of Growth / Marketing

*Secondary buyer. Appears at slightly larger or better-funded clients (Seed+/Series A with a first marketing hire) where the founder has delegated growth.*

---

## Overview

**Title(s):** Head of Growth, Head of Marketing, VP Marketing (early-stage), Growth Lead
**Typical seniority:** Director / VP
**Decision role:** Economic buyer (if founder has delegated budget) or influencer (if founder still approves)
**Found at companies:** B2B companies 15–50 employees, Seed / Series A, where founder brand is explicitly part of the growth plan.

---

## What They Care About

**Their primary metric:** Pipeline sourced from marketing, cost per qualified meeting, content engagement that ties to pipeline.

**Their biggest problem right now:** They've been told the founder brand is a growth lever, but they don't have the production capacity to make it happen. Their designer is stretched across product marketing; their writer is either a contractor or doesn't exist. Meanwhile, the founder keeps asking why the content isn't shipping.

**What a good week looks like:**
- Founder content ships on cadence without them personally producing it
- A carousel gets an inbound from a Tier 1 account
- The marketing dashboard shows founder-sourced pipeline as a category

**What a bad week looks like:**
- Founder is annoyed about content quality or cadence
- Competitor launches a carousel that looks twice as good
- Pipeline dips and the CMO/CEO asks "what's the content plan"

---

## How They Buy

**Involvement in purchase:** Initiates and influences; founder usually still approves the spend on their own brand.
**Discovery behavior:** Peer referrals (GTM Slack groups, Pavilion, Exit Five, RevGenius), LinkedIn, conferences.
**Evaluation style:** Analytical + social proof. Wants to see process, pricing, and case studies. Will ask about ICP targeting methodology.
**Common objections:**
- "Can you report on pipeline attribution?" → We track leads delivered and meetings booked; attribution beyond that is CRM-side and we plug into yours.
- "We have an in-house designer" → Great — we can slot into your brand system rather than fight it. The capacity constraint usually isn't design skill, it's available hours.
- "How do I trust the voice will match the founder?" → Founder reviews every piece before it ships. The first 2 weeks are voice calibration, not publishing.

---

## How to Reach Them

**Best channel:** LinkedIn (they live there professionally), warm intro through a GTM community is best, email second.
**Best time:** Monday–Wednesday. Fridays are dead for growth roles.
**What gets their attention:**
- Pipeline-attributable numbers, not vanity metrics
- Specific mention of their ICP and the signal methodology we'd use
- Reference to their founder's content specifically
**What gets ignored:**
- Vague "we do LinkedIn content" pitches
- Anything that sounds like it's selling to the founder and not to them

---

## Message Framework

**Value prop for this persona:**
Bolt-on founder-brand production + lead sourcing that ships on cadence and plugs into their existing growth stack — without hiring a content lead or designer.

**Proof points that resonate:**
- Concrete production cadence (e.g., 2 carousels/week + X leads/month)
- ICP methodology — how we target, enrich, and qualify
- Founder time required — specifically, how little
