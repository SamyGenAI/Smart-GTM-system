# Persona: Chief of Staff / Founder's Associate

*Gatekeeper and operator. Not the decision-maker, but often the one who screens vendors and runs the vendor relationship after close.*

---

## Overview

**Title(s):** Chief of Staff, Founder's Associate, Head of Operations, Executive Assistant (at smaller companies)
**Typical seniority:** IC / Manager, high trust with founder
**Decision role:** Influencer and gatekeeper. Can block a deal; rarely closes one alone.
**Found at companies:** B2B companies 10–40 employees where the founder has hired their first operations leverage.

---

## What They Care About

**Their primary metric:** Founder time freed up, vendor reliability, clean handoffs.

**Their biggest problem right now:** The founder keeps saying "I need to post more" but the content situation is a mess — they've tried a ghostwriter, a designer, and nothing has stuck. The CoS is now being asked to "just fix the content thing," and they need a vendor who won't require them to babysit.

**What a good week looks like:**
- Content ships on cadence without the CoS having to chase anyone
- Founder reviews and approves in under 30 minutes total for the week
- Weekly/monthly report arrives on time with clear numbers

**What a bad week looks like:**
- Vendor misses deadlines and CoS has to explain to founder
- Content requires 10 rounds of founder review
- Founder asks "is this actually working?" and there's no clean answer

---

## How They Buy

**Involvement in purchase:** Screens, runs the trial, manages ongoing relationship. Founder makes the final call.
**Discovery behavior:** Referrals from other CoS / ops peers, LinkedIn, operator communities (Chief of Staff Network, etc.).
**Evaluation style:** Process-driven. Wants to see onboarding plan, communication cadence, reporting format.
**Common objections:**
- "How much of the founder's time will this take?" → 30 min/week max after week 2. We design the process to protect founder time.
- "What's the onboarding process?" → Week 1 voice calibration + ICP definition, week 2 first drafts, week 3 first lead batch, week 4 full cadence.
- "What happens if the founder hates the first batch?" → First 2 weeks are calibration, not commitment. We iterate until the founder is proud to ship.

---

## How to Reach Them

**Best channel:** LinkedIn if they're public, email if not. Reference the founder by name and a specific piece of their content.
**Best time:** Tuesday–Thursday, mid-morning.
**What gets their attention:**
- Explicit commitment to low founder-time cost
- Clear process and reporting cadence
- A sense that we're a low-drama vendor
**What gets ignored:**
- Anything that sounds like it'll create more work for them
- Pitches that ignore them and go straight to the founder after they've replied

---

## Message Framework

**Value prop for this persona:**
A single, reliable vendor that ships founder content and qualified leads on cadence, with minimal founder time and a clean weekly report for you.

**Proof points that resonate:**
- Exact founder time requirement (in minutes/week)
- Sample weekly report format
- Onboarding timeline with week-by-week milestones
