# Persona: B2B Founder

*Primary buyer. 90% of Igentiv deals start and end with this person.*

---

## Overview

**Title(s):** Founder, Co-founder, CEO
**Typical seniority:** C-Suite (founder)
**Decision role:** Champion, economic buyer, and technical evaluator — the same person
**Found at companies:** B2B SaaS, B2B services, AI/dev tools, GTM tech. 1–30 employees, bootstrapped or Seed / Series A.

---

## What They Care About

**Their primary metric:** Pipeline and revenue. Secondarily, founder brand reach (followers, reply quality, inbound DMs from real buyers).

**Their biggest problem right now:** They know LinkedIn works — they've seen peers get meetings from it — but they can't sustain the cadence alone, their design looks mediocre next to the top 1%, and even when they post, it doesn't turn into pipeline. They're doing sales themselves and don't have a warm top-of-funnel.

**What a good week looks like:**
- Two high-quality carousels shipped that they're proud of
- 3–5 inbound DMs from actual ICP buyers
- One meeting booked from content or from leads delivered
- Zero hours they personally spent in Canva or writing drafts

**What a bad week looks like:**
- Haven't posted in 10 days, guilty about it
- The one post they did ship has 40 views and 2 likes
- Pipeline is dry, they're about to open Apollo and cold-email again
- Spent 4 hours on a carousel that still looks off

---

## How They Buy

**Involvement in purchase:** Initiates and approves. No procurement, no committee.
**Discovery behavior:** LinkedIn (following creators like Samy), Twitter/X, Substack, peer DMs ("who do you use for your posts?"), occasionally podcasts.
**Evaluation style:** Gut + social proof. Looks at the work first, the pitch second. If the agency's own brand doesn't look good, the conversation ends.
**Common objections:**
- "I was going to hire [Big Creator Agency]" → "Good shortlist. Honest question: are you buying their name or their work? If the name — they're the right choice. If the work — ask them how many clients per account manager and how long a revision takes. Then compare. One founder, direct Slack, turnaround in days, fraction of the price."
- "I can just do this with AI" → Show a side-by-side: same brief, generic AI vs. our workflow. The gap is obvious in 30 seconds.
- "I already have a ghostwriter" → Great — keep them. Carousels are the visual layer on top, and the leads are the bigger unlock anyway.
- "Price seems low — what's the catch?" → "No catch. I'm small, I run lean, I use AI where it makes sense. At $800–$1k/mo I'm the value option, not the expensive one. The tradeoff is you're not buying a big brand name — you're buying the work."
- "How do I know the leads will be good?" → We pre-qualify against the exact ICP you give us. First month is the proof.

---

## How to Reach Them

**Best channel:** LinkedIn DM (founder-to-founder from Samy), followed by email. Comments on their posts are a warmer entry than cold DMs.
**Best time:** Tuesday–Thursday mornings in their timezone. They check LinkedIn between meetings.
**What gets their attention:**
- Reference to a specific post they wrote (proof you actually read it)
- A visual example of what their ideas would look like as a carousel
- A peer founder's result — "X founder went from Y to Z in 60 days"
- Honesty about tradeoffs — founders can smell a pitch
**What gets ignored:**
- Generic "saw you on LinkedIn" openers
- Feature lists or deck-first pitches
- "Let me show you how AI can 10x your content" — they've heard it a thousand times
- Multi-paragraph cold emails
