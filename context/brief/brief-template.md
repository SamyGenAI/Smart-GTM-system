# Brief (compact context for outreach prompts)

Keep this file SHORT. Max ~150 words. This is the only context the enrichment prompt sees. Update this file, not the long positioning/ICP files, when you change the pitch.

## Offer
[YOUR_COMPANY]: [One sentence describing what you do and who you do it for. Focus on the outcome — what the client gets — not your process. Example: "We do X for Y so they can Z."]

## ICP
[Describe your ideal customer profile in 2-3 sentences. Include: role or founder type, company stage/size, geography, and the #1 signal that makes them a fit. Example: "Experienced B2B founders in Europe or US, 1-30 employees, Seed to Series A or profitable bootstrap. They [key behavioral signal]. [Qualifying detail — followers, revenue, team size, etc.]"]

## Anti-ICP
[List the types of leads to exclude. Be specific. Examples: creators without a real business, first-time founders, B2C, enterprise, founders who never post, etc.]
