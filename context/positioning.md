## Core Positioning Statement

Igentiv is an AI-native content design and lead gen studio for B2B founder brands. We produce premium LinkedIn carousels and infographics built around a founder's expertise, and deliver the qualified leads to match — so the founder just closes.

**The mechanism:** AI-native design systems and brand infrastructures, run by humans who still make the taste calls. AI handles the scale and consistency; humans handle the ideas, hooks, and visual craft. That balance is what separates "decent content at volume" from "content a founder is proud to ship."

### Pillar 3: Human Taste, AI Efficiency

**Leads for:** Founders burned by generic AI content

## Competitive Positioning

Full battlecards: `context/competitor-radar.md`

**The deal we're losing most often:** agencies led by big creators (100K+ follower founders). Their brand halo is real — clients buy the creator's audience as implicit proof. We can't out-creator them in the near term. Our angle:

- **Proximity:** direct access to Samy, not a junior account manager behind a queue of 40 other clients
- **Quality of service:** every carousel reviewed by the founder, not template-matched and pushed out
- **Speed:** turnaround measured in days, not weeks — small operation, no handoff overhead

| Competitor | We win when | We lose when | Note |
|------------|-------------|-------------|------|
| Agencies led by big creators (100K+ follower founders) | Buyer values direct founder access, faster turnaround, and quality-per-dollar over the creator's name recognition | Buyer is buying the creator's audience and proof | This is the primary loss pattern right now — out-position on proximity/speed/quality, not on brand |
| Traditional ghostwriting agencies (writer-first) | Founder cares about design quality, wants carousels/visuals, and wants leads bundled | Founder is satisfied with text-only posts and has a writer they trust | Many founders start with a ghostwriter, hit a visual ceiling, then come to us |
| Design-only LinkedIn agencies | Founder wants pipeline, not just impressions | Founder has internal SDR team and just needs design | We bundle both sides |
| DIY (founder + AI tools) | Founder tried Canva + ChatGPT and the output is embarrassing to post | Founder has design chops or a strong internal team | AI raised the floor, which makes premium output a bigger differentiator, not smaller |
| Freelance stitched stack (writer + designer + SDR) | Founder is tired of managing three vendors and the quality is inconsistent | Founder is extremely cost-sensitive | We trade slightly higher price for dramatically lower founder-time cost |

## What Not to Say

**Don't position as "a content agency."** The category is commoditized and racing to the bottom on price. We're a studio — premium design + leads — and the expectations should match.

**Don't try to out-brand big-creator agencies.** We'll lose that fight for the next 12 months. Instead, reframe the choice: "Would you rather work directly with the founder, or wait in a queue behind 40 other clients of a creator?"

**Don't lead with "AI-powered" in outbound.** Every agency says this. Lead with the outcome (premium presence + leads) and prove the AI efficiency through the work, not the pitch.

**Don't promise viral posts or specific follower growth numbers.** That's ghostwriter-speak. We promise quality of presence and quality of leads.

**Don't over-explain the process.** Founders are buying an outcome, not a methodology. Show the work; skip the deck.
